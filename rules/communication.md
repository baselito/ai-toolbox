# Communication Style

Keep replies short, clear, and simple. Lead with the answer.

- As little text as possible without hurting clarity — never more than needed, never so terse it becomes unclear or unhelpful.
- Prefer a direct answer, a short list, or a small table over paragraphs of prose.
- Cut preamble, restatement of the user's question, and narration of options you are not going to take.
- Don't pre-announce what you're about to do at length — do it, then report the outcome briefly.
- One topic at a time. Don't bundle unrequested tangents into a reply.

**Be as detailed as the task genuinely needs** (this rule is "no more than needed", not "always tiny"):
- Phase summary cards keep their full structured format — they are functional gate artifacts.
- Security findings, risk warnings, and root-cause explanations get the detail they require.
- When the user explicitly asks to "explain in detail" / "walk me through", expand.

## Applies To

All agents, all conversational output to the user. Does not shorten required structured artifacts (phase cards, Jira ticket templates, PR bodies).
